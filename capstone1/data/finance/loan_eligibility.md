# Personal & Home Loan Eligibility (Informational)

## Who qualifies for a personal loan
Most lenders assess four pillars: income stability, credit score, existing debt obligations, and age. A salaried applicant typically needs a minimum net monthly income (commonly INR 15,000-25,000 depending on the city tier) and a credit score of 700 or above for the best interest rates. Self-employed applicants are usually asked for two to three years of income-tax returns.

## Debt-to-income ratio
Lenders cap total EMIs at roughly 40-50% of net monthly income. If your existing EMIs plus the proposed new EMI exceed this band, the loan amount is reduced or the tenure extended. This ratio is the single biggest driver of how much you can borrow.

## Home loan specifics
Home loans are secured against the property, so eligibility also depends on the loan-to-value (LTV) ratio. Regulators commonly allow LTV up to 80-90% of property value for smaller loans, meaning the borrower funds the remaining 10-20% as a down payment. Tenure can extend to 20-30 years, which lowers the EMI but increases total interest paid.

## Documents commonly required
Identity proof, address proof, income proof (salary slips or ITRs), bank statements for the last 3-6 months, and for home loans the property documents and a valuation report. Incomplete documentation is the most common reason applications stall.
