# Regulatory FAQ: RBI & SEBI Basics (Informational)

## Who regulates what
The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) regulates banks, NBFCs, and payment systems and sets monetary policy. The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) regulates securities markets, mutual funds, and stock exchanges. Insurance is overseen by IRDAI. Knowing the regulator tells you where to escalate a complaint.

## KYC requirements
Know Your Customer (KYC) is mandatory before opening accounts or investing. It requires proof of identity and address. KYC can usually be completed digitally (e-KYC) using a government-issued identity number, subject to the institution's process.

## Grievance redressal
Banks must have an internal ombudsman; unresolved complaints can be escalated to the RBI Ombudsman scheme. For securities, SEBI runs the SCORES platform for investor complaints. Always get a complaint reference number for tracking.

## Investor protection note
Returns on market-linked products are never guaranteed. Any communication promising assured high returns on equity-linked products is a red flag and should be verified with the regulator before acting.
