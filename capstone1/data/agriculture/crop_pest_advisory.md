# Crop & Pest Advisory Basics (Advisory, not a guarantee)

## Integrated pest management (IPM)
IPM combines cultural, biological, and chemical controls to keep pests below damaging levels while limiting pesticide use. The sequence is: monitor and identify the pest, set an action threshold, prefer biological or cultural controls first, and use targeted pesticides only when thresholds are crossed. Blanket spraying on a calendar schedule is discouraged.

## Common rice pests
The brown planthopper and stem borer are major rice pests. Brown planthopper risk rises with excess nitrogen and dense canopies; managing nitrogen and maintaining gaps for airflow reduces buildup. Yellow stem borer damage shows as dead hearts in young crops and white ears at the panicle stage; resistant varieties and timely transplanting help.

## Wheat and common diseases
Wheat rusts (yellow, brown, black) are managed mainly through resistant varieties and timely sowing. Early detection of orange or brown pustules on leaves allows a targeted fungicide response. Crop rotation breaks disease cycles.

## Local variation note
Pest pressure, thresholds, and approved products vary by region, season, and crop stage. Always confirm the current recommendation and approved dosage with your local agricultural extension officer or Krishi Vigyan Kendra before applying any input.
