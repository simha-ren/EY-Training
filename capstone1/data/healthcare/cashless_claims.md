# Cashless Hospitalization Claim Process (Informational)

## What cashless means
In a cashless claim, the insurer settles the hospital bill directly with a network hospital, so the patient does not pay upfront (except non-covered items and any co-pay). It works only at hospitals empanelled with your insurer or third-party administrator (TPA).

## Planned admission steps
For a planned procedure, request pre-authorization at least 48-72 hours in advance. The hospital insurance desk submits the pre-auth form with the doctor's advice and estimated cost to the TPA. The TPA reviews and issues an approval letter for an approved amount. Carry your health card and a government photo ID.

## Emergency admission steps
In an emergency, the hospital initiates pre-authorization within 24 hours of admission. Treatment is not delayed for paperwork. If approval is pending, the hospital may take a refundable deposit that is adjusted once the claim is approved.

## Common reasons for partial approval
Room-rent limits, sub-limits on specific procedures, non-medical consumables, and waiting periods for pre-existing conditions can reduce the approved amount. The balance becomes payable by the patient at discharge. Review your policy schedule for these limits before admission.
