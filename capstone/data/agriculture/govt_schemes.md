# Government Agriculture Schemes Overview (Informational)

## Income support
Direct income-support schemes transfer a fixed amount to eligible landholding farmer families in installments through the year, paid directly to bank accounts. Eligibility is based on land records and an Aadhaar-linked, KYC-verified bank account. Exclusions apply to higher-income taxpayers and certain institutional landholders.

## Crop insurance
National crop insurance schemes protect against yield loss from natural calamities, pests, and disease for notified crops in notified areas. Farmers pay a small percentage of the sum insured as premium; the balance is subsidized. Claims are assessed against area-based yield data, so enrollment within the cut-off date and accurate crop declaration matter.

## Credit and Kisan Credit Card
The Kisan Credit Card (KCC) gives farmers short-term crop loans at concessional interest, with interest subvention for timely repayment. The card can also cover allied activities such as dairy and fisheries.

## How to check eligibility and apply
Most schemes are accessed through the official scheme portal, a Common Service Centre, or the local agriculture office, using land records and identity documents. Eligibility and benefit amounts change with policy revisions, so verify the current rules on the official portal before applying.
