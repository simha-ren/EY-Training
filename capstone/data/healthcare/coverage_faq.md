# Health Insurance Coverage FAQ (Informational)

## What is typically covered
Inpatient hospitalization (24+ hours), pre- and post-hospitalization expenses for a defined window, daycare procedures, and ambulance charges up to a limit are commonly covered. Always read the policy schedule for the exact list.

## Waiting periods
Most policies have an initial waiting period (often 30 days) during which only accident-related claims are paid. Specific conditions and pre-existing diseases carry longer waiting periods (commonly 2-4 years) before they are covered.

## Co-pay and sub-limits
A co-pay means the insured pays a fixed percentage of every claim. Sub-limits cap payouts for specific items such as room rent or particular procedures. Both reduce the out-of-pocket protection and should be checked before buying or upgrading a policy.

## Exclusions
Cosmetic procedures, most dental and vision care, and treatment arising from excluded activities are usually not covered. Non-disclosure of a known condition at purchase can lead to claim rejection, so disclose accurately.
