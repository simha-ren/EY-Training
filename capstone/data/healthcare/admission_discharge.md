# Hospital Admission and Discharge Process (Informational)

## At admission
The admitting desk records patient details, the treating doctor, and the provisional diagnosis, and assigns a room category. For insured patients, the insurance desk verifies the policy and starts pre-authorization. Keep a folder with the ID, policy card, doctor referral, and any prior medical records.

## During the stay
A case sheet tracks treatment, medication, and investigations. Interim bills are usually generated so families can track running costs. If the estimated cost will exceed the pre-approved amount, the hospital seeks an enhancement from the insurer.

## Discharge process
The treating doctor issues a discharge summary listing diagnosis, treatment given, and follow-up advice. For cashless cases, the final bill goes to the TPA for approval, which can take a few hours; the patient settles only the non-covered portion. Collect the discharge summary, final bill, pharmacy invoices, and investigation reports for records and any later reimbursement.

## Reimbursement alternative
If treatment was at a non-network hospital, the patient pays first and files a reimbursement claim with original bills, the discharge summary, and the claim form within the insurer's stated window (often 15-30 days of discharge).
