# National Millet Promotion & Farmer Resilience Programme — Project Proposal

## 1. Executive Summary
The National Millet Promotion & Farmer Resilience Programme (NMPFRP) is a
three-year initiative to increase millet cultivation across 12 districts,
improve smallholder farmer incomes, and strengthen climate resilience. The total
programme budget is INR 48 crore, co-funded 60% by the state government and 40%
by a development partner.

## 2. Objectives
- Increase area under millet cultivation by 35,000 hectares by Year 3.
- Raise participating farmer household income by at least 20%.
- Establish 60 farmer producer organisations (FPOs) and 12 processing units.

## 3. Eligibility
Support is available to small and marginal farmers holding up to 2 hectares of
rain-fed land. Priority is given to women-led households and farmers in
drought-prone blocks. Each eligible farmer receives an input subsidy of INR 5,000
per hectare, capped at 2 hectares per household.

## 4. Budget Breakdown
- Input subsidies to farmers: INR 21 crore
- FPO formation and capacity building: INR 9 crore
- Processing and storage infrastructure: INR 12 crore
- Monitoring, evaluation and administration: INR 6 crore

## 5. Timeline
Year 1 focuses on farmer mobilisation and FPO formation. Year 2 scales input
distribution and begins infrastructure construction. Year 3 completes processing
units and transitions FPOs to self-sustaining operations.

## 6. Key Risks and Mitigation
- Low farmer awareness: mitigated through 240 village-level demonstration plots.
- Erratic monsoon: mitigated by promoting drought-tolerant millet varieties.
- Market linkage failure: mitigated via buy-back agreements with three processors.

## 7. Monitoring and Evaluation
Progress is tracked against quarterly KPIs. An independent mid-term review occurs
at the end of Year 2. Success is defined as reaching 80% of the hectarage and
income targets.
